## ⚡ Executive Summary

I am a Software Engineer and AI developer studying **BSc Informatics at the University of South Africa (UNISA)**. My work lives at the intersection of **Computer Science** (robust algorithms and low-latency architectures), **Business Information Systems** (economic decision-support and process re-engineering), and **Human Experience** (accessibility, civic transparency, and social impact).

From industrial mineral processing models and real-time deepfake defense consoles to South African legislative verification tools and healthcare USSD queueing platforms, I build battle-tested, production-ready systems that solve high-stakes challenges.
