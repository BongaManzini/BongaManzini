## About me

I’m a BSc Informatics student from Pretoria, South Africa, building software where **technology meets real-world problems**. My work spans full-stack applications, data-driven research, cybersecurity, automation, and experimental interactive experiences.

I especially enjoy turning complex ideas into tools people can actually use — from smarter clinic queues and community finance ledgers to threat-intelligence dashboards and browser games.

- 🔭 Building: practical software, civic-tech tools, and security-focused systems
- 🧠 Exploring: AI/ML, distributed systems, data visualisation, and defensive security
- 🧪 Learning by: shipping prototypes, entering hackathons, and documenting research
- ⚡ Personal theme: **useful software, thoughtful design, measurable impact**
